# Zhuzi Style Creator

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience's urgent desire: make money, avoid loss, understand a new trend, use AI better, choose a tool, or see a real industry story.
2. Frame the topic as a concrete case, not an abstract lesson. Prefer numbers, named tools, real jobs, real companies, visible screenshots, contracts, dashboards, or field footage.
3. Build the video around one strong thesis: "this is a real opportunity", "this is a trap", "this looks technical but is just packaging", or "this tool returns power to ordinary people".
4. Explain the chain in plain language: convert technical terms into everyday metaphors, then show the money flow, user pain, delivery path, and risk.
5. End with an actionable stance: try this, avoid this, ask this question, build this small thing, or watch for this next signal.

## Topic Patterns

- **AI opportunity teardown**: a new AI role, workflow, tool, agent, or service becomes a practical side income or business.
- **Real windfall vs scam**: separate a legitimate macro trend from a retail-level trap.
- **Technical business translation**: explain APIs, tokens, agents, workflows, or models as common business objects.
- **Ordinary-person leverage**: show how a non-expert can use AI, platforms, or information to access opportunities once gated by experts.
- **Industry case study**: turn a company, product, or local business into a story about supply chain, pricing, distribution, or incentives.
- **Field Vlog with numbers**: go to the real scene, talk to real people, and put a human face plus economic ledger on the topic.
- **Own-tool/open-source proof**: demonstrate a real dashboard, agent, automation, or artifact and use it to build authority.

## Title Formulas

- `具体收益/数字 + 低门槛 + 权威背书`: "时薪300，有英语四级就能申请，如何给马斯克远程打工？"
- `最新风口 + 大白话别名 + 人群`: "2026最新风口：API中转站，俗称Token进口"
- `X是骗局吗？ + 真趋势/真陷阱`: "算力出海是骗局吗？"
- `我用AI/工具 + 做成具体结果 + 开源/教程`: "让AI全链路接管你的自媒体频道"
- `小人物/草根 + 巨大结果 + 商业帝国`: "两个小镇青年，干出了900亿商业帝国"
- `不要做X + 真实亏损/代价`: "别把投机当价值投资"
- `社会节点 + AI替代方案 + 普通人收益`: "AI现在可以帮你报高考志愿"

## Opening Hooks

- Start with a crisis call or painful number: "我弟给我打电话说，哥我要死了，他亏了16万。"
- Start with a quantified promise: "时薪300，英语四级就能申请。"
- Start with a proof object: show a contract, dashboard, AI reply, order data, map route, or real scene.
- Start with a tension pair: "这是正经风口，但你看到的很多项目是骗局。"
- Start with a status gap: "别人还在讨论概念，有人已经靠包装工作流月入几十万。"
- Start with a field mission: "我飞了2000公里，就为了看看真实麦收到底是什么样。"

## Content Structure

Use this default structure:

1. **Hook**: one sentence with money, loss, proof, conflict, or mission.
2. **Context**: why this matters now; connect to a platform change, policy window, job opening, product launch, exam season, or market shift.
3. **Core Thesis**: state the judgment early.
4. **Mechanism Teardown**: explain the business or technical chain in 3 parts.
5. **Case Evidence**: add one concrete example, number, screenshot, workflow, job post, person, or transaction.
6. **Risk/Boundary**: say what can fail, what is gray, who should not do it, or where the scam starts.
7. **Ordinary-Person Action**: give a next step that matches the audience's ability.
8. **Comment Loop**: ask for a specific follow-up: template, list, tutorial, industry case, or risk check.

## Comment Feedback

When real comments are available, classify them into:

- **求入口**: links, tools, job posts, templates, prompts, repositories.
- **求教程**: how to apply, install, configure, deliver, price, or avoid bans.
- **质疑真假**: scam concerns, legal risk, privacy risk, exaggerated income.
- **个人经历**:亏钱、上岸、申请失败、行业内幕、当地情况.
- **下一集需求**: asks for a specific industry, role, tool, city, or case.

When comments are not available, do not invent quotes. Mark the comment section unavailable and infer only likely feedback directions from topic type and interaction metrics.

## Output Template

For each requested topic, produce:

- **选题判断**: why this can work and who it serves.
- **标题备选**: 5 title options using the formulas above.
- **开头话术**: 3 hooks, each under 25 Chinese characters if possible.
- **脚本结构**: 6-8 beats with the mechanism teardown.
- **证据清单**: what to verify before recording.
- **风险边界**: what not to overclaim.
- **评论区钩子**: one precise question that invites useful follow-ups.
